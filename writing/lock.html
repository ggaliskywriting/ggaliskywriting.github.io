Lock an entry

Paste a piece of writing, pick a password, and this gives you a line to put in entries.js. The encrypting happens here in your browser — nothing is uploaded, and the readable words never end up in the published file.

Two things worth knowing.

There is no way to recover the writing if you forget the password — not for you, not for me. Keep the original somewhere safe until you're sure you still want it locked.

The date, title and tags of a locked entry stay visible to everyone. Only the writing is sealed. So give it a title you're happy for a stranger to read.
